BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Get ready

  1. Check your board. Use an original ESP32 development board with at least 4 MB flash. ESP32-S2/S3/C3 and other variants are not supported by this image.
  2. Check your display. Use exactly one 32 × 16 monochrome P10 HUB12 panel with 1/4 scan and matching scan order and polarity. White, red, green, blue, yellow or pink single-color variants may work; compatibility depends on the panel, not its LED color. RGB/multicolor and multiple panels are not supported.
  3. Connect a data USB cable. Plug the board into your computer. Close serial monitors and other programs using the port. Install the board's USB-to-serial driver if necessary.
  4. Check the wiring. The factory firmware enables the panel and GPIO26 buzzer output. Verify the pinout and power circuit before connecting them. For the official DevKitC, disconnect external board power before connecting USB.
First installation overwrites device data. This factory image overwrites the bootloader, partition table, OTA selection, app and the NVS area containing Wi-Fi credentials and settings, even if the installer's optional full-erase box is unchecked. It may make other stored data inaccessible. Back up anything needed and select the correct device.

Loading the installer and firmware…

This browser cannot access the serial port. Use Chrome or Edge on Windows or Ubuntu. Open this page over HTTPS or localhost to use serial access.

Your browser asks you to choose and authorize the serial port. In the dialog choose Install Momentra Clock, review the erase option and follow the prompts. Keep the cable connected until installation completes.

AFTER INSTALLING

Bring your clock online

  1. If the board does not restart automatically, press its EN / RESET button.
  2. On your phone or computer, join the open Wi-Fi network Momentra Clock. It has no password. If this network does not appear, see the troubleshooting section below.
  3. Open http://192.168.4.1/ in your browser. Open Wi-Fi setup, choose your home network, enter its password and select Save & reboot.
  4. Reconnect to your home network and try http://momentra.local/. If that does not work, find the clock's IP address in your router or serial logs. Allow time for Wi-Fi and NTP synchronization.
Use a trusted, isolated network. Setup Wi-Fi is open and unencrypted; nearby clients can observe submitted credentials. The dashboard and API have no authentication or encryption.

NEED A HAND?

Common questions

No serial port or board found

Try a known data cable and another USB port. Install the USB-to-serial driver for your board. On Ubuntu, check serial-port permissions and reconnect or sign in again after any permission change. Close other serial tools.

Connection fails or times out

Some boards need manual download mode: hold BOOT, press and release EN/RESET, then release BOOT; retry the connection. Board-specific sequences vary. Check the board manual.

The setup network does not appear

Wait for startup, press RESET and check power and serial logs. A device with retained valid credentials may connect to its saved network instead; this factory image normally overwrites NVS. Check the troubleshooting guide.

Can I modify the firmware?

Yes. Follow the build and upload guide to install ESP-IDF, edit the source, compile and flash your own build. Use the dashboard OTA feature only for a compatible application-only image.
