Momentra ESP Flasher

Install Momentra Clock or flash your own ESP firmware. Files and serial traffic stay on this computer.

1. Prepare and connect

Use Chrome or Edge and a USB data cable. Close serial monitors. On Ubuntu, serial-port permissions may be required. Some boards require holding BOOT while connecting.

Momentra requires an original ESP32 with 4 MB flash and one compatible 32 × 16 monochrome P10 HUB12 panel. Check wiring and power before connecting the display.

2. Choose firmware

Data loss: The factory image overwrites Wi-Fi credentials, alarms and other settings, even without whole-chip erase.

Choose binary files and offsets from your firmware provider. Each offset must be 4096-byte aligned. Only compatible ESP8266 and ESP32-family devices with a supported flasher stub and detectable flash can be used. Incorrect files or offsets can make a device unbootable.

Even without whole-chip erase, every sector touched by a selected file is erased and overwritten. Back up important data first.

3. Status and setup

Connect a device to begin.

After installing Momentra, connect to the open Momentra Clock Wi-Fi network, visit http://192.168.4.1/ and configure Wi-Fi. The setup network and dashboard are unencrypted and unauthenticated; use a trusted environment.
