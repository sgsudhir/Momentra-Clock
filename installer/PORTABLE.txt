Momentra ESP Flasher

Extract the entire archive, then launch momentra-flasher.exe on Windows or
momentra-flasher on 64-bit Ubuntu. If Ubuntu's file manager does not offer
Run, open a terminal in the extracted folder and run:

  chmod +x ./momentra-flasher
  ./momentra-flasher

Keep its window open. The launcher prints a local address and tries to open
your default browser. If necessary, copy the address into Chrome or Edge.
Internet access is not needed after download. Choose your USB serial device
when the browser asks for permission. Install the board's USB driver or grant
Ubuntu serial-port permissions if the device is not listed. Disconnect any
serial monitor. If connection fails, see the BOOT / RESET guide on the page.

The Momentra factory images support an original ESP32 or ESP32-S3 with at
least 4 MB flash. The S3 image uses the ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 N16R8 pin mapping;
check that the actual board and wiring match it. The installer selects the
image based on the detected chip. Both images overwrite saved settings and
Wi-Fi credentials even without whole-chip erase.
The custom mode also erases every flash sector touched by your selected files;
incorrect binaries or offsets can make a device unbootable. Back up first.

Momentra requires one compatible 32 x 16 monochrome P10 HUB12 panel. Check
power and wiring before use. After flashing and verification, release BOOT
and press EN / RESET once to start normally. Connect to the open "Momentra
Clock" Wi-Fi network and visit http://192.168.4.1/ to configure station Wi-Fi.
The setup network and dashboard are unencrypted and unauthenticated.
Provision on a trusted network. Press Ctrl+C or close the launcher to exit.
